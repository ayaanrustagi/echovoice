Install EchoVoice on iPhone

Until it's on the App Store, you can install the free beta yourself with your own Apple ID and a computer. It takes about ten minutes the first time.

Download EchoVoice.ipa iPhone or iPad · iOS 17 or later
  1. Get Sideloadly on your computerDownload it free from sideloadly.io for Mac or Windows. On Windows, also install iTunes and iCloud from Apple's website, not the Microsoft Store.
  2. Plug in your iPhoneUse a cable. Unlock the phone and tap Trust when it asks about this computer.
  3. Drop in EchoVoice.ipa and press StartDrag the file you downloaded into Sideloadly and enter your Apple ID. Sideloadly signs the app with your own account so your iPhone will accept it.
  4. Turn on Developer ModeOn the iPhone, open the setting below, switch it on, and restart when asked.
    Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode
  5. Trust the appOpen the setting below, tap your Apple ID, then tap Trust. EchoVoice is ready to open.
    Settings › General › VPN & Device Management
Good to know
  • With a free Apple ID, the app stops opening after 7 days. Plug in and press Start again to renew it. Your phrases and recordings stay on the phone.
  • NFC phrase tags may not work in this version, because free Apple IDs can't use that feature. Everything else does.
  • If someone you care for will use EchoVoice, it's easiest for a family member to do these steps.
