Terms of Use

Last updated November 3, 2026

Short version: EchoVoice is a free, open-source app built by three high school students. It is not a medical device and not an emergency service. Use it as a communication aid, and keep other ways to ask for help.

Who we are

EchoVoice is made by Ayaan Rustagi, Charan and Sree, students at Rouse High School in Leander, Texas. It is a student project, not a company.

Not a medical device

EchoVoice does not diagnose, screen, treat or monitor any condition. The voice journal compares a person only with their own earlier readings and is not a clinical measurement. It does not replace an AAC evaluation or advice from a speech-language pathologist or doctor.

Not an emergency service

The HELP screen and the five-blink alert are conveniences. They can fail, for example if the phone is off, muted, out of battery, or has no signal. Do not rely on EchoVoice as your only way to get urgent help, and make sure the people around you know another way to reach emergency services.

Free, beta, and sideloaded

EchoVoice is free. The current build is a beta that you install yourself with your own Apple ID, and it may have bugs. A free Apple ID stops the app opening after 7 days until you reinstall it, and your data stays on the phone. Sideloadly is a third-party tool, not made by us or Apple.
