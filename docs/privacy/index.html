Privacy Policy

EchoVoice does not collect personal data.

Plain-language summary: EchoVoice has no account, server, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Your phrases, recordings, personal information, and journal entries stay inside the app unless you choose to export them.

What EchoVoice stores

Phrases, voice recordings, voice-journal check-ins, settings, optional personal-information phrases, and on-device suggestion history are stored inside the app's private storage. EchoVoice does not upload or sync them. Your Apple device's operating-system backup settings may still include app data.

What EchoVoice does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No developer-operated servers or cloud database
  • No analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, or sale of data
  • No automatic sharing with the developer or any third party

Microphone and recordings

The microphone is used only when you choose to record a phrase or complete a voice-journal check-in. Audio recordings are saved on your device. EchoVoice does not receive them.

Speech recognition

During a voice-journal check-in, EchoVoice requests Apple's on-device speech recognition to compare the spoken practice sentence with its written text. EchoVoice requires on-device recognition and does not send the journal audio to the developer.

Camera and eye-gaze access

If you turn on eye tracking, the front TrueDepth camera is used to estimate gaze and blink gestures for hands-free selection. Camera frames are processed live on the device and are not saved or transmitted by EchoVoice.

NFC tags

EchoVoice can read and write compatible NFC tags at your request. A tag contains an EchoVoice identifier and the phrase needed to speak it. It does not contain an account, web tracker, or developer analytics identifier.

Texting your emergency contact

If your emergency contact includes a phone number, the HELP screen can open Apple's Messages app with a help text already written. You send it yourself by tapping Send. EchoVoice never sends a message on its own and cannot read your messages.

Personal Voice

If you grant access to Apple Personal Voice, speech is generated through Apple's protected system feature. EchoVoice does not receive or upload the Personal Voice model.

Exporting and deleting your data

Exporting a message-bank backup creates a file that you control and share only if you choose. You can remove saved phrases, recordings, personal information, and journal entries inside the app. Deleting EchoVoice removes its local app container, subject to copies retained by your device backups or files you previously exported.

Children

EchoVoice does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect data at all.

Changes to this policy

If EchoVoice's data practices change, this page and the date above will be updated before the change is released. The App Store privacy answers and the app's privacy manifest will be kept consistent with this policy.
