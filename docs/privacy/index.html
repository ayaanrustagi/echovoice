Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Short version: EchoVoice has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Your phrases, recordings and journal stay on your device unless you choose to export them.

What EchoVoice stores

Phrases, voice recordings, voice journal check-ins, settings, optional personal-information phrases, and on-device suggestion history are kept in the app's private storage. EchoVoice does not upload or sync them. Your device's own backup settings (iCloud or a computer backup) may still include app data, and we don't control those.

What EchoVoice does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No EchoVoice servers or cloud database
  • No analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, or sale of data
  • No automatic sharing with us or anyone else

Microphone and recordings

The microphone is used only when you record a phrase or do a voice journal check-in. Recordings are saved on your device. We never receive them.

Speech recognition

During a journal check-in, EchoVoice asks Apple's on-device speech recognition to compare what you said with the sentence you read. EchoVoice requires on-device recognition, and the audio is not sent to us.

Camera and eye gaze

If you turn on eye tracking, the front TrueDepth camera estimates gaze and blinks for hands-free selection. Frames are processed live on the device. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

NFC tags

EchoVoice can read and write NFC tags when you ask it to. A tag holds an EchoVoice identifier and the phrase to speak. Nothing else.

Help text to your emergency contact

If you add a phone number, the HELP screen can open Apple's Messages with a message already written. You send it yourself. EchoVoice never sends a message on its own and cannot read your messages.

Personal Voice

If you allow Apple Personal Voice, speech is made by Apple's protected system feature. EchoVoice never receives or uploads the voice model.

Exporting and deleting your data

A backup or a PDF/CSV journal export is a file you control and share only if you choose. You can delete phrases, recordings and journal entries inside the app. Deleting EchoVoice removes its local data, apart from device backups and files you already exported.

This website

This site is a static page on GitHub Pages. We add no cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub, which hosts it, may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Children

EchoVoice collects no data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Pilot program

If you join an EchoVoice pilot, the pilot has its own written consent form that says exactly what we ask you to share and how it is stored. Nothing is collected from the app itself. Taking part is optional and you can stop any time.

Changes

If our practices change, this page and its date will be updated before the change ships.
